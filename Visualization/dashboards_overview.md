# 📊 Dashboard Overview – Olist E-Commerce Analysis

This project consists of 6 focused dashboards, each designed to answer a specific business question and support data driven decision making.

## 1️⃣ Executive Overview Dashboard

### Purpose
Provide a high level snapshot of overall business performance and platform health for quick executive assessment.

### Key Metrics

• Total Revenue

• Total Orders

• Average Order Value (AOV)

• Average Review Score

• Cancellation Rate

### Key Insights

• The platform achieves a high average review score (4.1) despite operational challenges, indicating strong customer satisfaction overall.

• A low cancellation rate (0.6%) suggests generally healthy order fulfillment and system reliability.

2️⃣ Time & Trend Analysis Dashboard

### Purpose
Analyze how revenue and orders evolve over time to identify growth patterns, seasonality, and abnormal fluctuations.

### Key Insights

• November 2017 recorded the highest revenue and order volume, while December 2016 showed the weakest performance.

• After early volatility, MoM growth stabilizes starting January 2017, indicating platform maturity and operational consistency.

• Certain months (e.g., May) consistently outperform others, revealing seasonal demand patterns.

3️⃣ Geography Analysis Dashboard

### Purpose
Evaluate geographic demand supply balance by comparing customer concentration with seller distribution across states.

### Key Insights

• São Paulo dominates customer demand but suffers from a significant seller supply gap (customer-to-seller ratio ≈ 15.9), increasing delivery risk.

• Smaller states tend to show a healthier supply demand balance, suggesting lower operational pressure.

• Customer demand is not always aligned with seller density, highlighting opportunities for region specific expansion strategies.

4️⃣ Product & Category Analysis Dashboard

### Purpose
Assess category performance by combining revenue, order volume, and pricing to uncover hidden performance gaps.

### Key Insights

• High revenue does not necessarily mean high order volume; some categories generate strong revenue through higher prices rather than frequent purchases.

• Price positioning plays a key role in demand variation across categories.

5️⃣ Payment Behavior Dashboard

### Purpose
Analyze customer payment preferences and their impact on revenue and order value.

### Key Insights

• Credit cards dominate payment methods, reflecting customer preference for flexibility.

• Installment payments are associated with higher average order values, positively impacting total revenue.

6️⃣ Reviews & Delivery Experience

### Purpose

Analyze the relationship between delivery performance, order status, and customer satisfaction.

### Key Insights

• Longer delivery delays strongly correlate with lower review scores.

• Late deliveries consistently result in poor customer ratings.

• Order status impacts review behavior, with delivered orders receiving significantly higher scores than cancelled or delayed ones.

• The majority of customers are one time buyers, highlighting a significant retention opportunity

🔎 Overall Business Value

Together, these dashboards transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that support:

• Seller expansion planning

• Logistics optimization

• Customer retention strategies

• Revenue growth through pricing and payment optimization
